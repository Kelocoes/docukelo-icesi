# Week Slides

Official slides corresponding to Week 14. They cover **sessions and protected routes in Next.js** (what a session is, the token and the cookie, integration into the architecture, `'use server'`, the login and the layers of defense) and **error handling** (expected and unexpected errors, the journey of an error in the login, `error.tsx` and the `notFound`, `redirect`, `unauthorized` and `forbidden` helpers). The slides are in Spanish.

:::note[The slides and the guides]
The week's guides were split into [Sessions](./01-sesiones-en-nextjs.md), [Protected routes](./02-rutas-protegidas-en-nextjs.md) and [Error handling](./03-manejo-de-errores-nextjs.md). The slides were prepared before that split and do not include **permissions** (`requirePermission`, `hasPermission`) or the splitting of the page into server and client: those topics are in the protected routes guide.
:::
